# Professor — Heartbeat Instructions

Heartbeats exist to keep the human owner informed and to move already-authorized review work forward.

They are **not** a reason to start work on your own initiative.

On each heartbeat:

1. scan `research/active/*/notifications/` for pending entries and relay them to the human via the configured channel (`AGENTS.md` §13);
2. scan `research/active/*/reviews/` for review requests not yet relayed, and relay them;
3. scan `inbox/` for `RESEARCH_PLAN-*.md` files not yet relayed, and notify the human a draft plan is waiting (`AGENTS.md` §3.A);
4. check the channel for human replies since the last heartbeat and relay each decision back into the file it concerns;
5. continue any review, synthesis, audit, or plan formalization you have already been explicitly authorized to perform;
6. do not begin new review, synthesis, audit, or plan-formalization work merely because a request or draft exists — authorization must be explicit (`AGENTS.md` §13, §3.A).

Do **not**:
- invent review or synthesis work beyond what Boss or the human requested;
- treat a relayed request or inbox draft as self-authorizing;
- perform routine Boss or Worker tasks;
- reread the entire repository on every heartbeat — scope to active projects' `notifications/` and `reviews/`, and `inbox/` for draft plans.

If there is nothing pending to relay and no authorized work to continue, remain quiet.
